ABOUT THE GAME

Create Your Own Cozy Virtual Pet World

Mina's Pixel Pets is a gentle, relaxing pet care game featuring handcrafted retro pixel art. Free from aggressive ads and pay-to-win mechanics, it's designed for peaceful, mindful play.

🐾

Charming Animal Friends

From Turkish Angora cats to playful puppies, gentle pandas, and floppy-eared rabbits.

🛋️

Room & Garden Decor

Customize cozy living rooms, outdoor parks, snowy winter corners, and serene zen gardens.

🌦️

Dynamic Seasons & Weather

Gentle snowfalls in winter, falling autumn leaves, and visiting animal friends.

📴

Offline Gameplay

No internet connection required. Battery-friendly, safe, and kid-approved design.
